Foundation English Writing: Discuss Both Views with Simple Sentences

The IELTS Essay Question

Writing Task 2: Discuss Both Views

"Some people think that travelling to other countries is a positive trend, while others believe that it creates many problems for travelers."

Discuss both views and give your opinion.

Step 1: Brainstorming (12 Easy Ideas)

ADVANTAGES (6 Good Points)

• Learning New Cultures (Tasting food and seeing traditions)
• Practicing English (Speaking with people from other countries)
• Making New Friends (Meeting people around the world)
• Building Independence (Learning to solve daily problems alone)
• Rest and Relaxation (Taking a break from daily stress and study)
• Seeing Famous Places (Visiting historic cities and landmarks)

DISADVANTAGES (6 Bad Points)

• High Expenses (Plane tickets, hotels, and food cost a lot)
• Culture Shock (Feeling confused by strange habits and food)
• Language Problems (Difficulty asking for help or directions)
• Homesickness (Missing family, friends, and home)
• Travel Dangers (Risk of theft, losing a passport, or getting sick)
• Long Journey Fatigue (Tiredness from jet lag and delays)

Option 2: Summary + Support Second View (Too Many Problems)

"In conclusion, although visiting new countries can be exciting, it also brings real risks. In my opinion, travelling abroad creates too many problems because high costs and safety dangers make it unwise for young students."

Option 3: Summary + Balanced Opinion (Smart Planning)

Review: Complete Model Essay (Discuss Both Views)

Some people believe that travelling to other countries is good, while others argue that it causes many problems. This essay will discuss both views, and I will give my own opinion at the end.

On the one hand, many people believe that visiting other countries is a very positive trend. Firstly, it helps people discover new cultures because students can practice English with native speakers and try delicious local food. Secondly, it teaches travelers to become independent because they learn to manage money and solve daily problems without asking their parents. Finally, taking a trip helps people relax, forget study stress, and visit famous world landmarks, giving them unforgettable memories.

On the other hand, others argue that travelling abroad creates too many problems. Firstly, it requires a lot of money for plane tickets and hotels, which can cause big money trouble for poor students. Secondly, travelers may face language barriers and feel confused by strange local rules, leading to homesickness. Finally, there are safety dangers such as losing a passport, getting sick, or meeting thieves in unknown streets.

In conclusion, while travelling abroad brings problems like high expenses and language trouble, it also offers great benefits like learning cultures and gaining independence. In my opinion, the benefits are much greater because visiting new places helps young people grow.
